# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in the WebTerm community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of identity, background, experience or ability.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, specific and constructive.
- Assume good intent while addressing concrete impact.
- Accept responsibility, correct mistakes and learn from feedback.
- Keep private information, credentials and security reports confidential.
- Focus review comments on the work, never the person.

Harassment, threats, discrimination, sexualized attention, doxxing and sustained disruption are not acceptable in project spaces or when representing the project.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owners through GitHub. Security vulnerabilities must use the private process in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). Project owners may edit or remove contributions, warnings, access or participation when behavior is inappropriate. Reports will be reviewed as promptly and impartially as possible; privacy will be protected to the extent practical.

## Scope and attribution

This policy applies to all project spaces and public representation of WebTerm. It is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct.html).
