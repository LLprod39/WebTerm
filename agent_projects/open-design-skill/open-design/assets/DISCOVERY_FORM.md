# Discovery Form: Initial Brief

Please answer the following questions to help me define the design direction:

1. **Target Audience:** Who is this for? (Developers, Executives, Consumers?)
2. **Key Action:** What is the primary goal of this page/app?
3. **Tone & Feel:** Pick 3 words (e.g., Clean, Professional, Brutalist, Playful).
4. **Platform:** Web, Mobile, Desktop?
5. **Existing Brand:** Do you have a logo or primary color?
6. **Scale:** One-pager, Complex Dashboard, Landing Page?

---

### Visual Directions (If no brand provided)
- **Modern Minimal:** Clean typography, white space, subtle shadows.
- **Brutalist:** Bold strokes, high contrast, non-traditional layouts.
- **SaaS Dark:** Deep grays, vibrant accents, futuristic feel.
- **Soft UI:** Pastels, rounded corners, friendly vibe.
